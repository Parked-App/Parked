Support

We're a small team. We read every message.

For anything — a bug, a question, a feature request, or just to say hi — email hello@parked.app.

How do I start a session?

Pick a category and a timer (or leave it on "P" for stopwatch mode), then flip your phone face-down. Parked starts tracking automatically. Pick your phone up when you're done.

Sessions under 1 minute

Sessions shorter than a minute can't be saved — they're almost always an accidental brush of the phone, not a real focus session.

Calendar sync isn't working

Check that Parked has calendar access: open Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars → Parked, and make sure it's allowed. If you've just enabled calendar sync in the app, iOS may have asked for permission — make sure you tapped Allow.

Shortcuts aren't running

In the Shortcuts app, tap the shortcut you're using and make sure each action has "Show When Run" turned off. Otherwise iOS stops the shortcut to show a preview, which can't be dismissed while your phone is face-down.

Managing my subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel, open iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Parked. You'll keep access until the end of the current billing period.

Restoring a purchase

On the Parked Pro screen, tap "Restore Purchases." If you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe, your access will return immediately.

Where's my data stored?

All your sessions live on your iPhone. We never see them. You can export your history to CSV from the History screen if you want a backup.
