Parked

Focus by putting your phone down

Parked turns your phone's accelerometer into a focus timer. Flip it face-down to start, pick it up when you're done. Each session becomes a ticket with your category and notes — collectible proof of your focus.

How it works

Choose a category and timer, flip your phone face-down, and start working. Parked tracks how long it stays down. When you pick it up, you get a ticket with the time, category, and optional handwritten annotation. Sync to your calendar, share the tickets, or just collect them.

Features

• Categories with custom colors
• Live ticket preview with handwriting annotation
• Apple Calendar integration
• Shortcuts support
• History with search and export
• Completely private — nothing leaves your phone

Privacy

Your session history, preferences, and all annotations stay on your iPhone. We never collect, transmit, or store any of your data. Calendar syncs go straight to your own Apple Calendar using EventKit. No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
