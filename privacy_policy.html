Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Parked does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

Your focus session history, category names and colors, and all app preferences are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Calendar and Shortcuts

If you enable calendar sync, sessions are written directly to your own Apple Calendar using Apple's EventKit framework. We do not access or store your calendar data. If you enable Shortcuts, the app triggers shortcuts you have created in Apple's Shortcuts app. We do not see or store anything about those shortcuts.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment information, name, or email address associated with your Apple ID.

Analytics and tracking

Parked contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. No data leaves your device unless you explicitly export a session or sync it to your calendar.

Children

Parked does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.
